A deadpan one-line excuse from a robot vacuum after it ate a sock — no
hunger cliché, just a quiet relocation to somewhere warmer and darker.

Entry (116 characters):

> I didn't eat your sock. I relocated it somewhere warmer and darker, where it can finally be alone with its thoughts.

Run `python3 main.py` to write the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.
